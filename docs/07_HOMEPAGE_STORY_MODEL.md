# Lumyo Homepage Story Model

Version: 1.0

Status: Canonical

---

# Philosophy

The homepage is **not** a collection of independent sections.

It is a continuous cinematic experience where the visitor discovers Lumyo through a single uninterrupted journey.

Scrolling is never the objective.

Scrolling only advances the story.

The user should never perceive:

- page sections
- component boundaries
- independent blocks

Instead, every element should feel like part of the same universe.

---

# Narrative Principles

Every scene must satisfy five goals.

1.

Continue the previous scene.

Never restart the experience.

2.

Prepare the next scene.

Every scene must leave visual elements that justify what comes next.

3.

Never create visual discontinuity.

Nothing should suddenly appear.

Everything grows from something that already exists.

4.

The Robot is the origin.

Every visual element of the homepage ultimately originates from the robot.

5.

The Diamond is the destination.

Every visual transformation converges towards Lumyo.

# Story Chapters

## Chapter 1

Arrival

Purpose

Create curiosity.

The visitor understands there is something alive.

Main actor

Robot

Visual language

Stillness

Slow breathing

Ambient particles

Very subtle movement

No interaction yet.

---

## Chapter 2

Awakening

Purpose

Robot begins reacting to user presence.

Main actor

Robot

Events

Eye glow

Internal light

Energy pulse

Small movements

This scene should create emotional connection.

---

## Chapter 3

Transformation

Purpose

Robot stops being only a robot.

It becomes the origin of Lumyo.

Events

Light increases.

Energy accumulates.

Body begins dissolving.

Digital matter replaces physical matter.

---

## Chapter 4

Fiber Expansion

Purpose

Robot transforms into information.

Main actor

Optical fibers.

The fibers are not decoration.

They transport Lumyo.

Events

Birth of fibers

Fiber acceleration

Branch creation

Organic growth

Particles

Light propagation

---

## Chapter 5

Discovery

Purpose

This chapter contains three discoveries.

Discovery 1

Premium Websites

Discovery 2

Automation

Discovery 3

Artificial Intelligence

Important

Cards never appear.

Fibers build them.

Cards grow from information.

Glass expands.

Content fades in.

Icons illuminate.

---

## Chapter 6

Solutions

Purpose

Everything discovered so far becomes one ecosystem.

The three discoveries converge.

Visual language

Connections.

Flow.

Networks.

Motion.

Nothing disappears.

Everything converges.

---

## Chapter 7

Reveal Lumyo.

Not a transition.

A conclusion.

## Chapter 8

---

# Transition Rules

Nothing appears.

Everything grows.

Nothing disappears.

Everything transforms.

Nothing teleports.

Everything travels.

Nothing starts alone.

Everything has an origin.

Nothing ends.

Everything evolves.

---

# Animation Rules

Fast animations are forbidden.

Motion must feel:

Organic

Elegant

Premium

Confident

Large movements.

Long easing.

Continuous interpolation.

---

# Scroll Philosophy

Scroll does not reveal sections.

Scroll advances the narrative.

Every scroll position corresponds to a moment in the story.

The visitor is controlling time.

Not the page.

---

↓

Timeline

↓

State Machine

↓

↓

Narrative progression

---

# Future Expansion

Future homepage modules should only add new chapters.

The Story Model must remain the single source of truth for every visual experience implemented by Lumyo.
